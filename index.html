Blindspot PDF Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Blindspot PDF handles information when you use our app and related services. We respect your privacy and aim to keep the collection and use of personal data minimal and transparent.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide directly, such as feedback or support requests.
  • PDF files or document content you process through the app.
  • Device and app usage information, such as app version, operating system, and crash diagnostics.
  • Diagnostic or performance data needed to maintain reliability and improve the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the app experience.
  • Process PDF documents and related features requested by the user.
  • Diagnose bugs, troubleshoot issues, and improve app performance.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important updates.

3. Local Processing and Data Storage

Blindspot PDF may process documents locally on your device. We do not require users to upload sensitive files to a remote server in order to use core functionality. Where local storage is used, it is for the operation of the app and user convenience.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when necessary to support app operations, analytics, or technical maintenance. These providers are obligated to handle the information in a secure and lawful manner.

5. Analytics and Performance

We may use analytics tools to understand app usage patterns, performance, and user experience. This data is used in aggregated or de-identified form to improve our products and features.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You can choose not to provide certain information, though this may reduce the app's functionality. You may also manage permissions on your device and update or delete any local data associated with the app according to your device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Blindspot PDF is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after such changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through the support channels associated with the Blindspot PDF app or the repository owner listed for this project.
