Privacy Policy

Our Core Privacy Commitment

At BlindSpot, we believe your files and documents are strictly your business. BlindSpot operates on a strict 100% on-device, zero-cloud architecture. All PDF rendering, image processing, OCR, and redaction procedures happen exclusively on your device hardware.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because BlindSpot operates completely local and offline, we do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any of your data.

  • Documents & Images: Your files never leave your device.
  • Redacted Content: Names, emails, phone numbers, tax IDs, or custom terms remain transiently in memory and are never uploaded.
  • Personal Identifiers: No registration, phone numbers, or user IDs required.

2. Local Device Permissions

BlindSpot requests minimal standard platform permissions solely to fulfill its core redaction utility:

  • Storage / Files Access: Required to open local PDFs/images and save your redacted copies.
  • Camera / Photos (Optional): Required only if capturing a document photo directly within the app.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

BlindSpot includes zero third-party tracking SDKs, cloud AI APIs, or behavior monitoring tools. Native platform services (e.g., Apple App Store / Google Play In-App Purchases and Review dialogs) are handled securely through standard OS APIs.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our on-device privacy architecture, contact us at privacy@blindspotpdf.app.
