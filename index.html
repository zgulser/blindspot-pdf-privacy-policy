Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Our Core Privacy Commitment

At BlindSpot PDF, we believe that your documents are strictly your business. We built this application around a simple principle: your data should never leave your device.

BlindSpot PDF operates with zero-cloud dependency. All document processing, text analysis, and redactions happen entirely locally on your phone or tablet.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Document Files: We never upload, transfer, or store your PDFs or images on external servers.
  • Redacted Content: Names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, tax IDs, credit card numbers, or custom terms remain entirely in your device memory.
  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or device identifiers.

2. Local Device Permissions

  • Storage / File Access: Used exclusively to let you select a PDF or image from your local files and save the redacted copy back.
  • Camera / Photos (Optional): Used only if you capture a document image directly within the app for redaction.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

BlindSpot PDF does not include third-party tracking SDKs, advertising frameworks, remote OCR APIs, or behavior analytics tools.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding our privacy practices, contact us at: privacy@blindspotpdf.app
